## Integration

Original generated PNGs were copied to this directory. tools/build-dualblade-v2.cjs packs the reviewed source coordinates, filters disconnected matte fragments, removes distal painted blades, and emits explicit anatomy / weapon landmarks. Runtime applies faction and equipment palettes only to painted surfaces and draws equipped weapons at the new grip locations. Portraits and grade glints use the same body. No legacy outfit pixels or garment masks are loaded by the game.
