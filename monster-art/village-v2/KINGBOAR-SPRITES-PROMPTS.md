# Kingboar v2 runtime sprites

Mode: built-in image_gen, transparent alpha. Reference: kingboar-concept.png. Two 4-column × 4-row sheets combine into eight directions; rows: step A, idle, step B, melee attack.

## South through northeast

Use case: stylized-concept. Production sprite sheet for a 2D chibi wuxia RPG. The attached image is the approved Iron-armored Boar King model reference ONLY. Reproduce that exact creature: massive dark brown quadrupedal wild boar, silver-white bristle mane, ivory curved tusks, amber eyes, dark blue-black iron lamellar armor with antique bronze cloud motif edging, tiny jade forehead seal and restrained red cord tassels. Four cloven hooves, broad snout, short tail, no rider or weapon. Fine painted game art with clean readable silhouettes. Match the SAME individual, armor, color and scale in EVERY cell.
Create a SQUARE 1536x1536 sprite sheet on GENUINE TRANSPARENT ALPHA, exactly FOUR columns by FOUR rows, 16 separate full-body poses. Each cell 384x384; creature centered, entire body including tusks/tail/mane inside cell with at least 28px transparent padding, feet at consistent height. No ground shadow, no dust, no backgrounds, no labels, no grid, no text, no scenery, no isolated parts. Stable orthographic elevated 3/4 RPG camera with NO perspective size variation; facing rotation only changes direction. All rows in a column MUST face the column direction.
Rows TOP TO BOTTOM: row 1 slow walk contact pose with left front leg and right hind leg reaching forward; row 2 alert idle neutral weight on all four feet, head level; row 3 alternate slow walk contact pose with opposite pair reaching forward, legs visibly different from row 1; row 4 melee attack pose with head lowered and tusks thrust forward, shoulders braced and front legs planted. Body and armor retain same volume in every pose; no duplicated neutral poses in walking rows.
Columns LEFT TO RIGHT: 1 SOUTH = front view looking directly down toward viewer, snout centered and both tusks symmetric. 2 SOUTH-EAST = front-right three-quarter, snout toward lower-right. 3 EAST = true right side profile, snout strictly toward right edge. 4 NORTH-EAST = rear-right three-quarter, snout toward upper-right and rear nearer viewer. IMPORTANT: rotate anatomy and armor with the view, do not draw four copies of the reference angle.
