## Full lineup

Use case: stylized-concept.
Create a polished creature design lineup for the FIRST MAP of a Thai 2D chibi wuxia MMORPG, Little Immortal Village. Reference 1 is the redesigned environment: borrow jade, warm limestone, peach garden, bamboo, old timber palette. Reference 2 is the actual hero art: match its finely painted chibi game illustration, clean silhouette, rich readable material shading and small gold details. Do NOT include either reference as an inset and do NOT copy their UI.
Landscape 1536x1024, elegant warm ivory neutral studio backdrop, EXACTLY EIGHT distinct full-body creatures, two rows of four evenly spaced. No extra creature, no duplicate, no cropped tails/tusks, no title, no lettering, no numbers, no labels, no borders. Each subject occupies its own clear cell with ample blank gutter, soft modest contact shadow. Orthographic elevated 3/4 RPG camera; each creature faces slightly toward the viewer and screen right. The boss in bottom-right is clearly heavier and larger than normal boar but fits completely inside its cell. Threat escalates left to right, top to bottom. High quality appealing game art, formidable but no gore, no photorealism, not flat icons.
TOP ROW left to right:
1. Dew spirit: tiny round translucent pale aqua droplet body, two dark expressive eyes, short floating wispy arms, two little green leaf fins and a single suspended dew pearl; readable soft core, no human legs, no giant particle cloud.
2. Little poisonous mushroom demon: squat cream stalk creature, ruby-red mushroom cap with ivory spots, narrowed dark eyes under rim, tiny root feet and leaf belt, playful menace; small capped silhouette.
3. Sawtusk wild boar: compact stocky warm chestnut brown quadruped, dark jagged bristle ridge, two short serrated ivory tusks, broad snout, visible cloven hooves, NO armor; clearly a real stylized boar not a pig person.
4. Green grass serpent: slender jade-green coiled snake with pale yellow belly, subtle bamboo leaf pattern on scales, amber eyes, lifted triangular head; grounded coil silhouette, not a dragon, no horns or legs.
BOTTOM ROW left to right:
5. Moonshadow wolf: lean charcoal-indigo quadruped with silver lavender chest and eyebrow fur, crescent-shaped pale chest marking, cool violet eyes, bushy tail, restrained wisps only at tail tip; elegant clear canine silhouette.
6. Straw-hat bandit: chibi adult male outlaw with weathered woven conical straw hat, rough moss-brown layered wuxia clothing, muted rust-red sash, bare forearms, cloth wraps, simple curved iron sabre held safely outward, small stolen coin pouch; rugged and sly, no modern items, no excessive royal armor.
7. Black-striped mountain tiger: strong orange-gold tiger quadruped, clear black stripes, cream chin and chest, rounded feline ears, long black-ringed tail, amber eyes; lower broader stance than the wolf, no armor.
8. Iron-armored Boar King: huge muscular DARK BROWN BOAR on FOUR cloven hooves, tall SILVER-WHITE BRISTLE MANE, enormous paired curved IVORY TUSKS attached to lower jaw, angry AMBER eyes. Old blue-black IRON lamellar plates protect shoulders and upper back, restrained antique bronze cloud-pattern rims and one small jade seal on forehead. Scuffed real metal and leather straps. A few faded vermilion cord knots, no human helmet covering snout, no sword, no saddle, no rider, no wings, no reptile scales, no dragon antlers. Rounded chunky anatomy with heavy shoulders, threatening starter boss rather than cosmic endgame deity.
Keep the whole set visually coherent while preserving all eight distinct identities.

## Boss detail sheet

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production creature concept / model reference sheet for a chibi wuxia RPG first-map boss.
Redesign the Iron-armored Boar King, existing Lv25 boss, preserving its BOAR species. Reference 1 is the new starter-village environment palette/materials. Reference 2 is the hero illustration style and chibi proportions, not a character to reproduce.
Landscape 1536x1024, refined pale ivory parchment studio background, beautifully painted digital 2D game art, crisp silhouettes and controlled detailed shading. No text, no numbers, no labels, no decorative frame, no UI, no arrows.
Layout: left 60 percent a single LARGE full-body hero view of the boss facing screen right in elevated orthographic three-quarter camera, all feet and tusks visible. Right 40 percent three smaller separated studies vertically: TOP same boss rear three-quarter view showing the overlapping back armor and short tail; MIDDLE same boss lowered head, front hoof scraping the ground, weight wound up to charge, full body, no speed lines; BOTTOM two isolated material/detail studies side by side of its iron shoulder plate with cloud motif and jade forehead seal tied by a faded vermilion cord. Do not add unrelated creatures or human characters. Keep all studies separated with generous negative space.
Locked anatomy and design: massive stocky quadrupedal DARK BROWN wild boar, four stout legs with cloven hooves, extremely thick shoulders, broad unmistakeable boar snout and triangular ears. Tall sweeping SILVER-WHITE BRISTLE MANE from head to upper back, TWO HUGE curved IVORY TUSKS attached to lower jaw, gold amber narrowed eyes, short boar tail. Heavy overlapping BLUE-BLACK IRON lamellar plates over shoulder and upper back, slightly worn/scratched, sparse antique BRONZE cloud-pattern edges, leather belly straps, small jade forehead seal, sparse faded vermilion cord knots. Main body brown, mane silver, armor dark iron, ivory tusks; jade is a small accent, no glowing green body. Armor must leave face, tusks, legs and flanks readable. No human posture, no saddle, no rider, no dragon horns, no additional tusks, no reptile scales, no wings, no fantasy crystals, no giant magic particles, no gore.
Mood: territorial beast from the rocky northern lair near a serene jade-roof village, strong enough to be a first group boss, cute-stylized but imposing; not a divine endgame emperor. Materials and decoration match the village blacksmith's hand-forged iron and jade architecture. All views must show the same identity and equipment.
